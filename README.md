Extant translation operates inside a finite proteostasis envelope
(`B_total ≤ C_buffer`). A reduced dynamical model with saturable rescue and
superlinear aggregation feedback necessarily has a saddle-node collapse threshold;
two bounding arguments place E. coli roughly one order of magnitude inside that
envelope (×25 at the usage-weighted evaluation point, ×1.9 to ×25 across
chaperone availability θ). At that margin the distinguishing prediction is a
synthetic lethality reachable in wild type, without a sensitized background.
Separately, measured per-codon mistranslation is organized at the amino-acid
level, and survives regressing out mass-spectrometry sampling depth (z = −2.97,
p = 0.0026); on the translational-supply axis no structure is detectable even
when μ's own per-amino-acid pattern is imposed on it as the benchmark.

Not a code-origin claim.

Every number in the manuscript is regenerated by `envelope-paper/scripts/` from
`envelope-paper/data/raw/` and asserted by `envelope-paper/tests/` (103 tests).

## Two claims rejected on the evidence

Both appeared in `combined-paper/` and are excluded from the active manuscript,
which does not discuss them: they were withdrawn before submission and a reviewer
has no reason to read about them. They are documented for anyone reading the code,
in `envelope-paper/README.md` under *Archived analyses*, and reproduced by
`envelope-paper/scripts/07_removed_results.py`.

- **Metal-binding-site codon deployment.** A gene-level expression confound.
  Against non-metal positions of the same genes, 0 of 4 tests remain significant
  and His falls from OR 1.33 to OR 1.07. Roughly 40% of annotated sites are also
  mispositioned in sequence space.
- **Cross-species conservation of operational geometry.** Δ_A used E. coli μ for
  all three species, and 44 of 60 E. coli tAI values were bit-identical to the
  yeast values.
