# Rejected Components

This historical ledger prevents unsupported claims or invalid analyses from silently returning.

1. Corrupted tAI and cross-species vectors.
2. The confounded metal-site enrichment result.
3. The unsupported claim of 34 essential regimes.
4. Any direct redundancy-to-tolerance formula.
5. Additive mixing of quantities with incompatible units.
6. The universal claim that any saturable rescue model must fold.
7. Bistability or hysteresis attributed to the old one-variable ODE.
8. Numerical headroom estimates without a validated, traceable calibration.
9. The old codon-axis permutation analysis as evidence for the law.
10. A triplet-code proof of the law.
11. Cross-species Pareto geometry inferred from incomparable or compromised inputs.

These components are excluded from the canonical theory. Reconsideration requires new data or proof, explicit dimensional and confounding checks, reproducible analysis, and a new entry in `DECISIONS.md`.
