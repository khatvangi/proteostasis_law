# One integrated experiment: error-attributable DnaK client burden across ClpB capacity

**Status: design complete; no new wet-lab performed.** This is a conditional pilot-to-confirmation protocol, not a claim that unvalidated assays are ready for confirmatory measurement. A single system and endpoint are selected. G0–G6 below control execution; numerical calibration fields must be filled from the pilot before new confirmation cultures are started.

## 1. System and fixed factorial design

Use nonpathogenic *E. coli* K-12 MG1655 with chromosomal **ileS T243R/D342A** (ED) and a sequence-verified, isogenic repair back to editing-proficient **ileS** (REP). Keep native **clpB**, **dnaKJ/grpE**, **rpoH**, and **rpoS** intact. At the same neutral integration site introduce either one inducible additional wild-type **clpB** cassette (ADD) or the matched promoter/backbone without clpB (EMPTY). Use one IPTG-responsive expression system; carry the same regulator in every strain. Do not assume IPTG concentration equals functional capacity. Verify ClpB abundance and activity separately. No ATPase-dead construct is needed: it could poison native oligomers and introduce another mechanism.

Cross **2 alleles × 3 exposures (NONE/NVA/VAL) × 2 inducer levels (LOW/HIGH) × 2 cassettes = 24 cells per block**. LOW is zero added IPTG; HIGH is the lowest pilot concentration giving the locked functional increment without violating G2. Native ClpB remains a feedback-responsive baseline. EMPTY at both inducer levels estimates inducer effects, but cannot remove the metabolic cost of producing ClpB; G2 explicitly tests that limit.

All strains also carry the same low-expression, single-copy constitutive firefly luciferase reporter, the sole primary client. Luciferase provides a tractable native-activity readout; its DnaK association under this particular error perturbation is an assay-development question. The reporter is not the whole proteome. Verify its full sequence and copy number; quantify reporter protein and transcript independently of light output. Do not select another reporter after inspecting treatment effects. Failure of this client to provide measurable association stops this protocol's primary test.

Use three independent ED-to-REP construction lineages in the pilot, each contributing one complete 24-cell day block. A biological replicate is an independently inoculated culture; longitudinal aliquots, IP splits and MS injections are not replicates. Confirmation uses new independently inoculated cultures across days and balanced lineages, with n complete blocks determined by the variance rule in ANALYSIS_PLAN.md.

## 2. Environment, dose, and preparation

Use one chemically defined M9 formulation for every cell, with identical carbon source, salts, pH, osmolarity, aeration, temperature and amino-acid background. The published starting background is 100 µM each Ile, Leu and Val [S2, S4]; use one institutional M9 SOP with all additional ingredients recorded. Maintain fixed Lys/Arg concentrations for isotope switching; validate uptake in this prototrophic background. Proposed constant temperature is 30°C, with no heat shock. Culture vessel, fill volume and mixing must be fixed by the laboratory's validated aerobic SOP, not invented here.

Pilot doses are **additional 0, 0.1, 0.3 and 1 mM Nva or Val**; these are proposed screening concentrations, not established safe or effective doses. Screen growth, protein-specific synthesis and nascent errors first, without using DnaK-burden direction to choose doses. Select the lowest common Nva dose across both alleles and machine levels that passes G1/G2. Lock a Val dose separately by the same rule. Apply each selected exposure dose identically to ED and REP, ADD and EMPTY, LOW and HIGH. No condition-specific nutritional rescue, medium change or dose adjustment is allowed in confirmation. Val is a separate specificity intervention, not a presumed equal-error comparator. Match error burden across Nva/Val only if directly measured spectra and frequencies support it; otherwise report separate exposures.

Before the pilot obtain verified strains/cassettes, sequencing access, defined-medium ingredients, isotope-labelled and light Lys/Arg, suitable DnaK IP antibody and isotype reagent, reporter antibody, luciferase assay reagents, targeted-proteomics standards, and synthetic matched Ile/Val/Nva peptide standards. Obtain documented access to quantitative LC-MS/MS capable of resolving the required analytes, routine protein fractionation/immunoblotting and luminometry. Availability, instrument settings, antibody specificity and supplier performance are not assumed. Use approved facility SOPs; log versions, lots, calibration and sample requirements.

## 3. Longitudinal workflow

Sequence-check allele repair, cassette junctions and reporter; assess off-target/background differences by whole-genome comparison where available. Carry construction lineage as a block factor. Validate reporter and strain growth against their unmodified parent as a pilot qualification, not as a replacement for REP controls.

Pre-equilibrate cultures in the common medium. Induce extra ClpB before exposure until the pilot-locked abundance plateau; use the same elapsed induction time in EMPTY. Start exposure at a fixed exponential growth window. Record a pre-exposure specimen at **−1 min**, add NONE vehicle, Nva or Val at **0 min**, and pulse with heavy Lys/Arg from **20–25 min**. Chase with the same total light amino-acid composition while maintaining exposure and inducer. The isotope-switch procedure and handling occur in every cell, including NONE; verify that exchange itself does not trigger stress. Freeze exact pulse/chase exchange and precursor enrichment criteria at G1.

Collect chase specimens at **0, 2, 5, 10, 20 and 40 min after the pulse ends** (exposure times 25, 27, 30, 35, 45, 65 min). These times are a proposed pilot grid, locked before confirmation; if capture dead time or precursor clearance makes them unusable, revise during pilot and regenerate the entire balanced sheet before freezing. A 40-min window does not establish infinite-time clearance. Residual cohort at the last point is censored, not extrapolated to zero.

At each specimen rapidly partition matched aliquots for input proteomics, DnaK IP and isotype IP, soluble/insoluble recovery, reporter activity, and stress markers; obtain cell counts/biomass and growth measurements. Stabilize DnaK interactions at sampling using a facility-validated rapid quench/capture procedure. Validate ex vivo binding/release, lysis timing, input recovery and fixation effects with mixing controls and non-fixed comparisons during pilot. If preserved in vivo association cannot be supported, label the readout capture-defined and fail G3 for a mechanistic primary claim. Keep total longitudinal withdrawal below the pilot-validated volume limit; otherwise use matched terminal sister cultures and record them as a clustered unit, never extra n.

Randomize culture position, exposure assignment, harvest order and analytical order within balanced blocks; stagger start times to preserve exact ages. Archive the allocation seed before inoculation. Blind specimen IDs for IP, MS, activity and QC interpretation; a separate allocation file is held until exclusions and calibration are locked. Include pooled reference material and process blanks in every analytical batch; never confound treatment with batch or alphabetical replicate order.

## 4. Complete assay matrix and interpretation

| Measurement | Specimen/time | Required control/normalization | What it establishes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nascent substitutions | Pulse-end input; repeat new short pulse in separate matched aliquot at late chase if precursor/recycling warrants | Cognate and error peptide standards; site localization; decoys/FDR; response factors; measured precursor enrichment; DNA sequence | Newly synthesized reporter and endogenous-peptide error frequencies, by substitution/site, with detection limits; not error counts inferred from added dose |
| Protein-specific synthesis | Heavy reporter and a locked endogenous panel at pulse end, plus total incorporation | Absolute/relative peptide calibration, pulse duration, cell equivalents, precursor enrichment | Molecules produced per cell per time; total abundance alone is insufficient |
| DnaK client burden | All chase times, input + DnaK IP + isotype | Reporter cohort amount, recovered DnaK, spike recovery, isotype subtraction, standardized cell equivalents | Captured DnaK-associated cohort fraction and its time integral; association stock, not throughput or dwell |
| Machine and expression | Baseline, pulse end, last chase | ClpB/DnaK/DnaJ/GrpE protein; clpB and reporter transcript; EMPTY inducer controls | Actual machine abundance, independent reporter expression and feedback; not capacity from transcript alone |
| Functional ClpB check | Pilot and batch reference extracts | ATP-dependent luciferase aggregate-reactivation assay, ATP omission and matched EMPTY extract; standard substrate/input | Functional increment of the DnaK/ClpB system; expression without function fails G2; lysate activity is not in vivo capacity |
| Aggregates | Input, soluble and insoluble fractions at all times | Recovery/mass balance, pellet washing, contamination markers, cell equivalents | Accumulated insoluble reporter and proteome stock; cannot alone measure formation flux |
| Degradation | Total labelled reporter across soluble + pellet fractions, all times | Dilution, cell division, isotope recycling and extraction recovery | Cohort disappearance compatible with degradation only after competing losses bounded; no disappearance-to-folding shortcut |
| Native function | Luciferase activity and absolute reporter abundance at all times | Matrix-spike recovery, linearity, substrate excess, baseline old pool and error-dependent specific activity | Total reporter activity; pulse-cohort functional yield UNIDENTIFIABLE without the independent validation in §5, not total chaperone turnover |
| Stress and physiology | Baseline, pulse end and last chase (retain other aliquots) | RpoH and RpoS protein; dnaK/groEL and katE transcript panel; growth, viable counts, morphology | Feedback timing and general stress; no causal RpoS mediation claim without a separate perturbation |

Val and Nva are isomeric; ordinary precursor mass alone cannot distinguish them. Require chromatographic/fragmentation validation against both synthetic standards for the same peptide sequences. Report unresolved Ile→(Val/Nva) as combined, not falsely specific. Measure non-Ile substitutions in the same nascent search and distinguish DNA variants, chemical modifications and sequence errors. Failure to resolve the primary Nva spectrum or to recover enough nascent signal fails G1; reporter light loss is never an error-frequency assay.

## 5. Primary observable, limits and gates

For culture c, let Bc(t) be background-subtracted, recovery-standardized heavy reporter recovered with DnaK, divided by its labelled input amount at chase zero. Define **Yc = trapezoidal integral from 0 to 40 min of Bc(t)**, in captured-cohort-fraction·min. Use equal cell equivalents relative to the original culture and correct growth dilution. Report raw recovery and B values; negative background-corrected estimates remain in the uncertainty calculation, not clipped. DnaK normalization corrects analytical recovery only: do not normalize away biological increases in DnaK abundance.

Let D(m,k) = [Y(ED,NVA,m,k)−Y(ED,NONE,m,k)]−[Y(REP,NVA,m,k)−Y(REP,NONE,m,k)]. The primary factorial estimand is **Theta = [D(HIGH,ADD)−D(LOW,ADD)]−[D(HIGH,EMPTY)−D(LOW,EMPTY)]**. Conditional hypothesis: functional extra ClpB lowers this error-attributable association integral, **Theta < −delta**. Transient transfer from aggregates can increase DnaK association; this is a test of the selected sign, not a universal prediction about ClpB. Val uses the same contrast as a secondary specificity check.

**True occupied site-time primary outcome is not identifiable here.** IP detects co-associated clients, including possible indirect complexes; it does not count occupied substrate-binding sites or distinguish rebinding from long dwell. The selected mechanistic observable is only admissible if G3 passes. Even perfect cohort decay gives an apparent residence/clearance time, not microscopic dwell. Without calibrated binding multiplicity/capture, the only robust site-time bound is 0 to the integral of measured total available DnaK sites over the window, conditional on site-count calibration. No positive lower bound or infinite-window upper bound is claimed.

Total activity and pulse-labeled abundance are separately measurable. **Pulse-cohort functional yield is UNIDENTIFIABLE** without an independent pulse-cohort activity assay or a validated conditional synthesis-stop preserving physiology. Unlabeled newly synthesized reporter after the pulse contributes to total activity, alongside surviving pre-pulse reporter and the labeled cohort; error variants may also differ in specific activity. Total activity therefore cannot be assigned to the pulse cohort or converted to its active-molecule count from abundance alone. Any conditional synthesis-stop must independently preserve the relevant error, synthesis, growth, stress and chaperone physiology; stopping synthesis is not assumed neutral or authorized as an unvalidated confirmation change. A cohort-specific functional assay also needs validated error-dependent specific-activity calibration before claiming new-native molecule yield. Report total function and pulse-labeled abundance separately. Aggregate loss, DnaK release and degradation do not demonstrate productive folding.

| Gate | Exact decision rule |
|---|---|
| G0 provenance | Public processed data remain non-confirmatory; confirmatory cultures must be new and excluded from pilot tuning. No old gate is reinterpreted. |
| G1 fidelity | Sequence identity and assay standards pass; nascent error increase has a positive lower 95% CI in ED NVA vs ED NONE and an ED-specific exposure increment; primary spectrum is distinguishable. Otherwise no causal-error primary. |
| G2 separability | Functional ADD HIGH increment is positive with a lower 95% CI; equivalence CIs for machine-induced differences in nascent errors/spectrum, reporter synthesis, growth and REP toxicity lie inside independently locked margins. Otherwise only total perturbation effects are interpretable. |
| G3 measurement | Capture specificity/preservation, linear range, blank, mass-balance and precursor QC pass frozen numerical limits; primary reporter association above validated quantification limit. Failure means primary mechanistic observable not identifiable; do not substitute aggregates. |
| G4 freeze/power | Pilot-only variance, delta, equivalence margins, doses, timings, panel, QC limits, n and allocation/code hashes frozen before new confirmatory cultures. If feasible n cannot resolve delta, stop for feasibility. |
| G5 decision | All earlier gates pass: upper 95% CI(Theta)<−delta supports the bounded sign hypothesis; lower CI>−delta rejects a reduction of at least delta; lower CI>0 is an opposite-sign falsifier. Otherwise inconclusive. |
| G6 function | Reduction in association is called productive relief only if pulse-cohort functional yield is identifiable through an independent pulse-cohort activity assay or a validated conditional synthesis-stop preserving physiology, with validated error-dependent specific activity, an independently supported increase in new-native yield and no explanation solely by synthesis suppression, degradation, death or failed recovery. Otherwise association relief only; no new-native yield claim when pulse-cohort functional yield is UNIDENTIFIABLE. |

No hidden change of endpoint after controls are viewed. Use ordinary institutional BSL-1 practice for K-12 work, strain verification, PPE, aerosol minimization, decontamination and biological waste handling; follow institutional chemical/isotope and recombinant-DNA procedures. No pathogenic, environmental-release, or sensitive operational work is included.
