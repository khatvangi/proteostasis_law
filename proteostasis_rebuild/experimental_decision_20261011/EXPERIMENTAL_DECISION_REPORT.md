## One system, one experiment, bounded inference

Select isogenic MG1655 IleRS T243R/D342A versus repaired IleRS, NONE/Nva/Val exposure, and inducible extra ClpB versus its matched empty cassette at two inducer levels. Retain native ClpB. This directly connects to the published system while allowing a measured functional increment instead of interpreting a knockout as a pure capacity dial. The full design has 24 cells per block. A single-copy luciferase client supplies a matched synthesis, association and functional readout; bulk aggregates and stress markers provide context. The complete assay matrix, collection sequence, specimen times and reagent classes appear in protocol §4; they are binding parts of this report, not alternatives.

Primary **Theta** is the repaired-allele-corrected Nva increment in time-integrated DnaK-associated nascent reporter across machine dose, additionally corrected for inducer effects in EMPTY (protocol §5). Its uncertainty comes from independent culture blocks. It is a defensible assay-defined observable only after capture validation. It is **not occupied DnaK site-time**. True dwell/site-time remains unidentified; only explicit physical upper bounds are permitted. Neither capture enrichment nor aggregate stock is processing flux, and neither processing nor clearance is productive native yield.

The central causal weakness is that allele×Nva effects can include metabolism beyond errors, and added ClpB can change growth, translation and feedback. Repair and no-Nva controls remove simple main effects, not every interaction. Direct nascent error spectra, synthesis and physiology equivalence are therefore go/no-go conditions, not nuisance variables adjusted away after failure. If those conditions fail, report only total genotype/exposure/machine effects. No strong error-mediated causal claim follows from a significant factorial coefficient alone.

The pilot uses three independent construction lineages/day blocks, with dose and inducer calibration before the complete assay block. It estimates technical feasibility and variance, not confirmatory effect size. Sample size and equivalence margins remain unknown until calibration; numerical values are not fabricated. G0–G4 require qualified assays and a locked design before new confirmation cultures. G5 supplies exact CI-based support, rejection and inconclusive rules; G6 separates association relief from productive relief. A null or opposite-sign result cannot be rescued with aggregates, a different client, or a Val-only result.
