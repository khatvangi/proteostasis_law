## Problem boundary

At **fixed, measured protein-specific synthesis rates** and a specified strain, medium, temperature and growth phase: what additional processing does translation error cause beyond error-free folding and degradation, which machine pools does it occupy, and how does changing machinery alter that load and client function? Measure errors, client outcomes, occupancy and activity independently. Do not define load by observed failure or infer capacity from survival. Ordinary folding/degradation is the baseline, not zero demand.

Outside scope: entire-cell demand, a definition of viability or life, genetic-code origin, new network/feedback extensions, and new theory branches. Synonymous codons remain a secondary perturbation of timing or error probability, requiring separate measurements of each.
