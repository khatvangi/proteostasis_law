# Adversarial audit of the assembled reassessment

11 October 2026. This is a self-audit of the delivered files, not an independent review or a new biological result. Verdict: coherent and reproducible as a constrained theoretical reassessment; **not submission-ready** as original empirical research.

## Actual weaknesses

1. **The main estimand is unobserved.** Equations define causal error-attributable processing, but no experiment measures it. Matching synthesis does not make an antibiotic an error-only intervention. An orthogonal perturbation or fidelity rescue plus injury controls is required. This is the central empirical deficit, not a missing confidence interval on an otherwise estimated burden.
2. **Steady site-time requires an environment and exit convention.** Little's law applies to consistent visits and residence, including dilution/loss in the model. A finite-window pulse measurement must handle boundary censoring and induction; it cannot be inserted uncorrected into the stationary expression. Competition and feedback alter w, so increments measured at fixed environment cannot automatically be added across perturbations.
3. **The simple model omits real constraints.** One site per chain, effective degradation, no machine degradation beyond dilution, no assembly intermediate and one machine pool are explicit simplifications. Positivity and conservation do not make these biological approximations accurate. The model is an accounting realization, not a comprehensive proteostasis simulator.
4. **Function is not inferred from conformation.** Mutant native protein can have altered activity. The native-output identity cannot provide an essential quota or a death boundary. The manuscript requires independent activity and does not turn a shortfall into lethality.
5. **The external negative test remains negative.** The Fauvet direction is opposite the archived kinetic model's usual direction. Membrane contamination and unmatched dilution may explain part of the difference, but neither explanation has been demonstrated. No correction by parameter tuning was made. The manuscript states point-estimate failure without claiming statistical refutation.
6. **Supplement availability was misstated in old records.** Later Fauvet tables are present. They provide per-protein means/SDs, not the missing total-contrast covariance or individual replicate totals. The delivery repairs the blanket retrieval claim locally, without changing earlier records. Summing SDs in quadrature would assume independence and is not used.
7. **Prior art is extensive.** FoldEcoSlim already combines background proteome, growth and σ32 regulation; FoldEco, Santra and FoldME cover further elements. Correct equations and a clean causal definition do not establish enough novelty for a research article. No “first” claim survives.
8. **Retrospective agreement is not validation.** Rescue/cooperation observations informed the model. Selected simulation signs and their passing unit tests are not new experiments. The old ensemble counts are attributed to saved outputs, not described as rerun here.
9. **Primary-source access is uneven.** Some facts are supported at abstract or main-text level; Calloni/Kerner client tables and Babu scores remain unavailable in the existing ledger. V04's duplicate-publication flag needs resolution. Citation IDs identify sources; they do not erase access limits.
10. **The prospective alternatives are operational, not a newly proven mechanism.** The δB cutoff is a prespecified assay effect size, not a physiological threshold. Without assay variance, effect size, allocation and sample size, the proposal is not a ready laboratory protocol. Intervals spanning δB decide neither hypothesis. A measured effect on one machine cannot be generalized to the entire proteome.

## What publication could and could not claim

**Could claim, with these limitations retained:** corrected bookkeeping and conditional identities; a records-based reassessment of an unsupported quantitative envelope claim; specific retrospective experimental constraints; and a transparent, statistically unresolved negative model confrontation. The author list is preserved, but this does not certify author approval.

**Could not claim:** measured translation-error workload, a calibrated physiological margin, universal collapse, established saturation, new experimental validation, a new finite-capacity network or feedback mechanism, a generally beneficial chaperone response, universal synonymous protection, or a statistically proven Fauvet refutation. No editorial acceptance or novelty verdict has been obtained.

Mathematical checks can certify their stated algebra and examples only. The source manifest and evidence matrix make the claim trail inspectable; they cannot create missing experimental measurements. Final publishing decision remains **NO-GO / not submission-ready**. STOP: no additional theory branch is required for this bounded task.
