# Decision gates

Date: 2026-10-11. Prospective tests only; none run for this reset. Supported records motivate the tests; contribution sizes and interaction signs remain conjectural.

Use matched strain, medium, temperature and phase; measure protein-specific synthesis and dilution in every arm. Prespecify the primary readout, effect size worth detecting, interaction scale and replicate-based uncertainty. A null excludes only effects within the assay’s power. No further simulations substitute for these measurements.

| Gate | Discriminator and minimum proof/data | Stop rule |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Error-attributable increment** | Induce mistranslation versus matched control at fixed measured synthesis. Directly quantify nascent substitution frequency/spectrum, client folding/activity, pulse-chase degradation and aggregation. Include an orthogonal error perturbation or fidelity rescue to separate errors from drug injury; ordinary error-free folding/degradation is the counterfactual baseline. Require a reproducible downstream increment attributable to the measured error change. | If synthesis/other injury cannot be separated, do not call the difference translation-error workload. If a powered contrast is null, reject a substantial increment under that condition. |
| **2. Ordinary client folding demand** | At matched synthesis and measured error frequency, compare a documented folding-prone client with a matched client/control. Measure client-specific machine association/residence, native activity and turnover. Require differential occupied site-time, not just a list of binders or an insoluble fraction. This separates normal folding demand from error induction. | Without occupancy or turnover, stop quantitative demand claims. Binding alone establishes neither obligation nor productive rescue. |
| **3. Finite occupancy and σ32 response** | During a calibrated load pulse, measure total/free/bound DnaK and GroEL, client arrival/residence, active σ32 reporter and later functional machine abundance. Reconcile free+bound with total and compare intact feedback with independently controlled machinery expression. Require temporal evidence separating early occupancy from later induction; check synthesis changes. | σ32 alone is not an error meter or measured headroom. Abundance alone is not throughput. Stop capacity-margin claims without phase-matched flux × residence data and independently demonstrated service limits. |
| **4. Injury × machinery capacity** | Factorial control/injury × baseline/altered capacity, with injury type fixed (prefer gate 1’s calibrated error induction). Independently verify functional capacity, synthesis and errors in all four arms. For a prespecified damage readout `y`, estimate `I=y11−y10−y01+y00` with uncertainty; report client activity separately. Require occupancy/routing evidence to attribute an interaction to machinery competition. | A nonzero interaction alone proves neither saturation nor a threshold. If capacity alteration changes error induction or synthesis inseparably, stop that mechanistic attribution. A powered null rejects the proposed interaction in the tested range, not finite pools. |
| **5. Existing held-out contradiction** | Retain Fauvet’s **ΔdnaKJ versus ΔdnaKJ ΔhtpG** aggregation comparison as an external check. The current model predicts an increase after htpG deletion; recorded 30 °C insoluble fractions decrease **20.5% → 18.9%**. Minimum resolution: the five-replicate values/uncertainty, membrane-versus-aggregate accounting, matched 30 °C growth/synthesis, and client function. Growth suppression results at 37/39 °C do not resolve 30 °C aggregation. | Until resolved, stop calling the kinetic model experimentally validated. If the opposite sign is robust under matched conditions, reject its signed aggregation prediction. Do not tune to this endpoint and continue calling it held out. |

## Secondary synonymous-codon test

Only after identifying a measurable contribution, use isogenic synonymous variants with unchanged intended amino-acid sequence. Match/measure transcript abundance, synthesis, elongation timing and substitutions; repeat the same client occupancy/activity readouts. A timing effect is not evidence of mistranslation, and a substitution contrast is not evidence of misfolding. The recorded mDHFR/GroEL result supports this secondary test, not a general codon-to-error-to-burden claim ([claim decisions lines 33–34](../experiment_grounded_rebuild_20261010/theory/CLAIM_DECISIONS.md#L33)). Stop broad codon claims if those mediators cannot be distinguished.

No universal threshold, in-vivo headroom, all-client recovery requirement or novelty claim follows from passing these gates. The records presently establish neither the error-attributable workload nor a calibrated capacity margin; the Fauvet point-estimate contradiction remains unresolved.
