## Definitions and three conditional results

A mistranslation is amino-acid misincorporation relative to the encoded sequence. A synonymous DNA change preserves that sequence while potentially changing initiation, elongation, mRNA structure or abundance. Neither slow nor fast translation is itself mistranslation. Error-containing chains need not misfold; correctly translated chains can require chaperones.

Let X_a be disjoint states of one specified client, measured as chain equivalents per volume, including aggregate material counted by constituent chains. Let s be its synthesis rate, μ the dilution rate, and d_a irreversible loss constants. Let M be concentration of functional service sites of one machine, F its free sites and B occupied sites. Functional sites are an explicit model quantity, not automatically the measured concentration of chaperone subunits. All three results are necessary or conditional statements; none estimates a biological threshold.

**Result 1: source conservation constrains steady-state losses.** Assume internal transitions conserve client chain equivalents, all states are counted once, and external inputs are s. Define P=Σ_a X_a. Summing state equations cancels every internal transfer once as an input and once as an output:

    dP/dt = s − μP − Σ_a d_a X_a.
    Steady state: s = μP + Σ_a d_a X_a ≥ μP.

Thus P≤s/μ for μ>0. With bounded s≤s_max and constant μ>0, integrating the differential inequality gives P(t)≤P(0)e^(−μt)+(s_max/μ)(1−e^(−μt)). This is the proof; aggregation alone cannot create chain mass. With μ=0, zero degradation and positive s, no finite steady state exists. With μ=0 and a removal route, a steady state may exist. Neither case establishes a universal biological collapse transition. Growth arrest also invalidates casually estimating synthesis as μP.

**Result 2: a finite stationary processing pool bounds admitted workload.** Let b_i count admitted binding visits of class i per volume per time, ν_i sites occupied per visit, and τ_i its mean residence time. Repeated visits count separately. Assume stationary balanced conditions, finite means, consistent volume normalization and negligible long-window boundary terms; dilution/loss must be included in residence accounting. Integrating occupied sites over a long interval counts the same site-time as summing the durations of all admitted visits. Dividing by observation duration and volume and taking the stationary limit yields

    B = Σ_i b_i ν_i τ_i ≤ M,       F = M − B ≥ 0.

For the first-order concentration realization, dC_i/dt=b_i−q_i C_i with q_i including all exits and dilution, C_i=b_i/q_i and τ_i=1/q_i. Multiplying by ν_i and summing proves the same identity directly. For an outside supply λ_i, λ_i can replace b_i only if all offered visits are actually admitted without uncounted abandonment, bypass or accumulation. That extra assumption is not supplied by these papers. If independent productive yield per visit is y_i, productive output is b_i y_i, not B. Small occupancy may accompany poor rescue; full occupancy may accompany successful throughput. Occupancy bookkeeping without measured arrivals and residence is not biological validation.

**Result 3: competition follows under fixed shared-pool kinetics, not merely finiteness.** Consider distinct clients with one site per bound chain, clamped free concentrations U_i, fixed positive exit rates q_i and capture rates k_i F U_i. Write C_i in occupied-site units and γ=1 site/chain for the explicit binding conversion. Its balance is dC_i/dt=γ k_i F U_i−q_i C_i. At binding steady state set a_i=γ k_i U_i/q_i. Then C_i=a_i F and conservation gives

    F=M/(1+Σ_l a_l),       C_i=M a_i/(1+Σ_l a_l).
    For k≠i: ∂C_i/∂a_k=−M a_i/(1+Σ_l a_l)²<0 if M a_i>0.

If productive chain release J_i=f_i C_i/γ with fixed f_i>0, its derivative has the same sign. This proves displacement under shared binding, fixed machinery, nonzero occupancy and fixed client kinetics. Separate pools produce no such derivative. Feedback increasing M, altered q_i, or changes in U_i can reverse the total response. In particular, if synthesis fixes a client's required eventual exit flux, increased competition can instead increase its free backlog; clamped-U reasoning cannot be silently transferred to that system. The derivative is a conditional local statement, not a universal sign of chaperone deletion.

**Dimensional audit.** X and P have units chains/volume; s and μP have chains/volume/time. M,F,B have sites/volume. b has visits/volume/time, ν has sites/visit and τ has time; bντ therefore has sites/volume. In the one-site realization, k has volume/sites/time, so kFU is chains/volume/time with the one-chain binding conversion explicit. q and f have inverse time; a=γkU/q is dimensionless, since γ has sites/chain. Abundance divided by an unknown residence time is not measured capacity. Aggregate percentage, sigma32 induction and binding occupancy cannot be added. The numerical checks use arbitrary algebraic witnesses, never parameter values asserted to describe a cell.

**Prediction B: selected nonsynonymous mDHFR variants have higher tabulated spontaneous refolding rates than WT.** For the complete eleven-variant panel in published Table 1, the descriptive prediction is k1_variant>k1_WT for every row.
